WHY NEXT EXISTS

Students don't lack opportunities. They lack a clear way to discover and navigate them.

University students discover opportunities through university portals, emails, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp groups, friends, company websites and student communities. At the same time, they may not know which opportunities actually connect to their interests or future career.

01
THE PROBLEM

Information exists.
Direction gets lost.

Students are not necessarily missing opportunities because there are none. The problem is that useful information is fragmented, difficult to compare and often disconnected from career direction.

University emails LinkedIn Instagram WhatsApp Company sites Student communities Event platforms
02 — MINI RESEARCH

What students told us.

We surveyed 15 university students about how they discover opportunities, how often they miss them and how confident they feel about their career direction.

13/15 had found out about an opportunity after the deadline or event had passed.
8/15 check 3–5 different places when looking for opportunities.
3.87/5 average confidence in knowing what career roles their degree can lead to.
3.93/5 average confidence in knowing what skills to develop for the career they want.
01 Opportunities arrive from everywhere.

Students mentioned university portals, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp, friends, event platforms and other sources.

02 Finding the right opportunity is difficult.

Students mentioned not knowing where to look, finding opportunities too late and not knowing which opportunities are relevant.

03 Career direction is still uncertain.

Several responses asked for clearer career guidance, skill information, mentorship and easier access to relevant opportunities.

Research sample: 15 university students · September 2026
