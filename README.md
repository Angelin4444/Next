# ✦ NEXT - Discover. Decide. Grow.

## The Problem

### Students don't lack opportunities. They lack a clear way to discover and navigate them.

University students can find opportunities through university portals, emails, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp groups, friends, company websites, event platforms, and student communities.

The problem is that this information is **scattered across too many places**.

Students often have to search through multiple platforms to find opportunities, compare what is relevant, and understand whether an opportunity connects to their career goals. As a result, useful opportunities can be discovered too late, while students may still be unsure about which career direction to pursue.

### What our research showed

We conducted a mini survey with **15 university students** in September 2026 to understand how students discover opportunities and how confident they feel about their career direction.

* **13/15** students had discovered an opportunity after its deadline or event had already passed.
* **8/15** students check **3–5 different places** when looking for opportunities.
* Students reported difficulties with knowing **where to look**, finding opportunities **on time**, and deciding **which opportunities are relevant**.
* **3.87/5** was the average confidence in knowing what career roles their degree can lead to.
* **3.93/5** was the average confidence in knowing what skills to develop for their desired career.

> **The information exists. The direction is what gets lost.**

---

## The Solution

### One place to discover what's next.

**NEXT** is a student-focused web platform that brings **opportunity discovery and career exploration together**.

Instead of making students search across multiple platforms, NEXT creates a clearer journey from discovering an opportunity to understanding how it can contribute to their future.

### How NEXT works

**01 — DISCOVER**
Find internships, hackathons, competitions, workshops, career fairs, and other opportunities in one place.

**02 — UNDERSTAND**
Explore career paths based on interests and understand the skills, roles, and opportunities connected to them.

**03 — ACT**
Take the next step by accessing the official opportunity or resource and turning information into action.

### The Journey

**Discover an opportunity → Build a skill → Gain experience → Understand your direction → Move closer to a career goal**

NEXT is designed to reduce the time students spend **looking everywhere** and help them spend more time **moving forward**.

---

## Why NEXT?

NEXT does not aim to replace existing opportunity platforms.

Instead, it acts as a **clearer starting point** — bringing scattered opportunities and career information into one student-friendly experience.

> **NEXT — Discover. Decide. Grow.**
